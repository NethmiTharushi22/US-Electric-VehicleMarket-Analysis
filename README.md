US Electric Vehicle Market Analysis
Overview

This project analyzes the distribution of vehicle fuel types across U.S. states to understand electric vehicle adoption, compare market share among fuel categories, and identify regions that would benefit most from future EV infrastructure investments.

The project combines SQL for data preparation and analysis with Power BI for interactive dashboard development.

Objectives
Clean and validate vehicle registration data.
Calculate EV, PHEV, HEV, and gasoline market share by state.
Identify states with the highest and lowest EV adoption.
Compare California with other major states.
Analyze the significance of alternative fuel types.
Provide infrastructure investment recommendations using data-driven insights.

Key Insights
California records the highest EV adoption rate.
District of Columbia and Hawaii also demonstrate strong EV adoption.
Gasoline remains the dominant vehicle fuel nationwide.
Hydrogen vehicles currently represent a niche market.
Recommendations
Expand EV charging infrastructure in high-adoption states.
Increase incentives in states with low EV adoption.
Continue investing in charging networks to support future EV growth.
